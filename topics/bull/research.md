# Bulls and red (sources)
- Cattle are dichromats, red-green colorblind. (Jacobs et al. 1998, Visual Neuroscience)
- Charge triggered by movement; tested with colored capes and dummies. (MythBusters 2007 "Red Rag to a Bull"; Smithsonian)
- Red muleta: tradition, masks bloodstains. (Britannica "bullfighting")
