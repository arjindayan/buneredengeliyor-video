# GTA — born from a bug (research)
- Working title Race'n'Chase; development began 4 April 1995 at DMA Design, Dundee (Wikipedia: Grand Theft Auto (video game)). Player was a police officer chasing criminals; considered dull, switched to playing as criminal (Wikipedia, Gary Penn quote "nobody wants to be the cop").
- Police route-finding bug made cop cars too aggressive, ramming the player instead of blocking; Penn: "That was awesome, so that stayed in" (Destructoid 2023 citing Gamasutra interview).
- Unstable, "abysmal" car handling, almost canned (Destructoid); "numerous attempts to halt development" (Wikipedia).
- Released 28 Nov 1997 Europe, MS-DOS/Windows (Wikipedia). Pre-launch violence controversy exploited by publicist Max Clifford as free promotion (Wikipedia).
- GTA VI release: Thursday 19 November 2026 (Rockstar on X, Nov 2025 delay notice; Forbes May 2026; multiple outlets Nov 2026 "not delayed again"). As of 13 Nov 2026 not yet out.
