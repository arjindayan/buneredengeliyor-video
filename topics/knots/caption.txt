Why do ships measure speed in knots? ⚓⏳

Sailors threw a wooden board on a rope off the stern, flipped a sand glass, and counted the knots that slid through their hands. Their count was the speed. The unit stuck, and one knot is still exactly one nautical mile per hour (1.852 km/h). And never say "knots per hour"!
