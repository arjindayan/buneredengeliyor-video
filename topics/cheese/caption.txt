Old photos didn't skip the smile because of long exposures. It was manners. 🧀📸

A wide grin once looked silly, even drunk. A London photographer had sitters say "prunes" for a tiny mouth. Then cheap Kodak cameras made photos fun, and by 1943 "just say cheese" was in a Texas newspaper.

Say it now and feel your lips stretch 😁
