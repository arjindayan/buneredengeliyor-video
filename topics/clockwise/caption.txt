Every clock you own is secretly copying a shadow ☀️🕰️

North of the equator, a sundial's shadow sweeps west → north → east. The first mechanical clocks just copied it. Down in Australia, the shadow turns the other way, and a few rebel clocks in Florence and Prague still run backwards.
