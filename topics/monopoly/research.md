# Monopoly: verified claims (History.com article by L. Gandhi; British Library blog by S. Campion 2023)
- Lizzie Magie, Henry George follower; Landlord's Game patent granted Jan 1904 (US 748,626). Critique of monopolists; Monopolist and Prosperity rule sets (BL: refined later to two sets; History: in patent description).
- Spread via college economics classes and Quakers; Quakers fixed prices and used Atlantic City street names (BL).
- Charles Darrow sold to Parker Brothers 1935; gave a false account of origins (BL). Parker paid Magie $500 for patent, no royalties (BL).
- Magie nearly forgotten until 1970s; Ralph Anspach's Anti-Monopoly case (1973 onwards) uncovered history.
- Not claimed: Darrow payment amount (unsourced). "First board game millionaire" is BL only, left out.
