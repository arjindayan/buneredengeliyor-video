Monopoly was built to protest getting rich, not celebrate it. 🎩

In 1904, Lizzie Magie patented The Landlord's Game to show how landlords get richer while renters get stuck. She even wrote two rule sets: one where everybody wins together, one where you crush everyone else.

Homemade copies spread through colleges, a man named Charles Darrow sold a version to Parker Brothers in 1935, and Lizzie got $500 for her patent. No royalties.

Her story only resurfaced after a 1973 lawsuit.
