Why are school buses always yellow? 🚌💛

It wasn't a coincidence. In April 1939, a professor named Frank Cyr gathered officials from all 48 states, bus builders and paint experts at Columbia University. They set 44 national standards, and one was the color. The reason given: black lettering stands out best on yellow, even at dawn and dusk.
