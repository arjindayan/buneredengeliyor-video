# Bless you fact check
- Heart does not stop in a sneeze; pressure change can alter rhythm, felt as a "skipped" beat (Library of Congress Everyday Mysteries, 2024; cardiologist R. Conti quoted). Heart-stopping idea = folk belief (Wikipedia "God bless you" cites Snopes/Straight Dope).
- Pope Gregory I / plague 590 story: popular, but Wikipedia calls it apocryphal; LOC says only "it is believed". Script: "historians doubt".
- Custom already established by c. AD 750 (Wikipedia citing Whiting 1977).
- Soul-as-breath / evil spirit entry explanation: folk explanation (LOC, Wikipedia "some have offered"); script says "many people believed".
- Cut: 100 mph / 5 ft sneeze figures (uncited), Pliny, Banish the Omen (unconfirmed).
- "thousand-year-old" = 750 AD to today ≈ 1,270 yrs; safe.
