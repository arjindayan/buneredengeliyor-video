# Berries (sources)
- Botanical berry: fleshy fruit from a single flower with one ovary, seeds in the flesh. Bananas, grapes, tomatoes, kiwis, eggplants qualify. (Britannica "berry"; Stanford Magazine "Strawberries aren't berries")
- Strawberry is an accessory fruit; "seeds" are achenes, each a fruit with one seed. (Britannica "strawberry")
- Raspberries/blackberries are aggregate fruits. Pumpkins/watermelons are pepos, a modified berry. (Britannica "pepo")
